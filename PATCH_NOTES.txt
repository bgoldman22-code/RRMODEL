Debug Logging Patch for Schedule Loader

Modified:
- netlify/functions/nfl-predictions-generate2/getRealScheduleForWeek.js

What it does:
- Logs the schedule fetch URL, HTTP status, raw counts, and mapped counts.
- Normalizes schedule from .matchups first, then falls back to .games/.schedule.
- Uses getTeamAbbreviation() so team keys align with NFLVerse data.
- Emits clear logs for fallback usage and errors.

How to use:
1) Unzip into your repo root.
2) Commit & push.
3) Clear cache & deploy in Netlify.
4) Trigger:
   /.netlify/functions/nfl-predictions-generate2?season=2025&week=3&force=1
5) Check function logs in Netlify for lines starting with [sched].
