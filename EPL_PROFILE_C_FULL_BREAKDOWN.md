## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS & TAKEAWAYS

### 1. Market Inefficiency Pattern
**The market underprices BTTS NO in the 0.27-0.53 probability range.** When the model predicts low-to-medium BTTS likelihood, bookmakers offer odds that overestimate BTTS probability, creating profitable NO opportunities.

### 2. Efficiency at Extremes
**Markets are efficient at high BTTS probabilities (>0.70).** Despite model showing 15-20% edges, BTTS YES bets at high confidence barely break even. Sharp money compresses these lines.

### 3. Sample Size Validation
**Large samples confirm profitability.** The three best bands have 22-35 matches each, demonstrating consistency beyond variance. Combined top 3 bands: 79 matches, 17.36 units profit.

### 4. Kelly Criterion Guidance
- **Kelly > 0.30:** Aggressive opportunity (Bands: 0.27-0.41)
- **Kelly 0.15-0.30:** Moderate bet sizing (Bands: 0.39-0.51, 0.33-0.43, BTTS YES)
- **Kelly < 0.15:** Small bets or skip (Bands: 0.43-0.53)

### 5. Hit Rate Requirements
- **BTTS NO at 2.15 odds:** Need 46.5% breakeven, achieving 50-59% = profit
- **BTTS YES at 1.70 odds:** Need 58.8% breakeven, achieving 60%+ = marginal profit
- **Lower odds require higher accuracy** - explains why BTTS YES struggles

### 6. Edge vs. ROI Relationship
**Large edge doesn't guarantee high ROI due to variance and sample size.** Bands with 19-26% edge (0.25-0.37, 0.72-0.84) show mixed results, while moderate 8-14% edge bands (0.35-0.53) deliver consistent profits.

### 7. Promoted Team Impact
**125 matches (27%) involve promoted teams with neutral ratings.** This likely suppresses overall performance slightly, suggesting profitability may increase when all teams have established ratings.
